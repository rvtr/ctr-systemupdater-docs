All known SystemUpdaters for CTR

WARNING: Some SystemUpdaters are from the gigaleaks and may not work as expected:

  • 0.10.2 and earlier are in a prototype format and will not install or run.
  • 0.13.0 and earlier use a ROFS container for RomFS which is not supported by newer firmwares. This may also apply to some firmwares after 0.13.0.
  • 0.14.6, 0.14.8, and 0.14.12 come in two types. Retail which will not work on development units, and dev which will work.

    • All version info has been gathered from the official changelog, gigaleaks, and online dumps. Updaters in green have been released, while updaters in red remain undumped.

    From note 127-54 of the SDK release info mentions that the RegionSelector app was removed. RegionSelector was used to change to the US or Europe regions. While no specific SystemUpdater version is mentioned in that note, the README from 0.14.24 also mentions removing RegionSelector. I'm assuming 0.14.24 is the first introduction of separate US and Europe SystemUpdaters, so all entries prior to then have been blanked out.

    Note 188-17 mentions adding support for Chinese, Tawiwanese, and Korean markets in SystemUpdater 0.16.64. Since 0.16.64 is also the first introduction of Chinese, Korean, and Taiwanese in my archives, entries for these regions before then have been blanked out.
